# DM template bank, per segment

**These are scaffolds, not scripts.** The hard rule still holds: no two DMs go
out identical. Before sending, swap every `[bracket]` for something real and
specific you actually saw on their profile or posts, and cut anything that
doesn't fit. If a template feels copy-pasted, rewrite it in your own words.

**Write like a human, not a bot.** Before sending ANY message, run it through the
rules in `.opencode/skills/x-growth/voice.md` ("Human writing"). Specifically:

- **No em dashes or en dashes.** They read as AI. Use a period, a comma, or just
  split into two short sentences.
- No AI tells: "delve", "elevate", "unlock", "seamless", "in today's world",
  "I hope this finds you well", tidy three-item lists, over-polished grammar.
- Lowercase-casual is good. A typo-free, perfectly balanced sentence looks
  generated. Keep it loose and real.

**Brand handle:** whenever you mention the product, use **@trypitchdotco** (the
official handle), not just "PITCH". Helps them find and follow it.

Use the lightest opener that fits. For high-fit prospects (`score >= 8`), default
to the free-demo opener, it's the strongest. Keep DMs short. Always be upfront
that you're with @trypitchdotco. The free done-for-them demo is a warm opener,
not a limitation: users can also sign up and try @trypitchdotco themselves at
https://trypitch.co. Mention that when they ask for a link, seem hands-on, or
would rather explore without waiting on you.

Placeholders: `[name]` `[product]` `[specific_post]` `[specific_detail]`
`[their_pain]` `[link]`.

**Free-demo opener (preferred)**
> hey [name], saw you shipped [specific_post]. the [specific_detail] is slick.
> i'm with @trypitchdotco, we turn a written walkthrough into a narrated demo
> video. [product] looked perfect to test it on so i made you a quick 30s demo,
> no strings, it's yours: [link]. what would you change about it?

## follow-up (silent prospect, ONE only, about 3 to 4 days later)

> hey [name], no worries if it's not a fit or just bad timing. leaving the
> [demo or offer] here in case it's useful: [link]. either way, [genuine
> specific compliment or well-wish on their work]. 👋

*After this, stop. Move to `lost`. Re-engage later via likes only, never more DMs.*

## reply if they push back

Pull the matching response from the `x-outreach` skill's SKILL.md ("Objection
handling") and put it in your own words for their specific objection. Never
argue. "not interested" or "stop" means `do-not-contact`, immediately.
